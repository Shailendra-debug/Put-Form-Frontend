Section 01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit it. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your login state, your preferred theme, or how you navigate between pages.

Cookies are not programs and cannot run code on your device. They cannot access other files on your computer or read data stored by other websites. Most cookies are identified by a name and a value, and they are sent back to the originating server with each request.

Cookies vs. similar technologies

In this policy, "cookies" also covers similar technologies we may use, including:

  • Local storage — browser storage used to keep preferences such as your chosen color theme.
  • Session storage — temporary storage cleared automatically when you close the browser tab.
  • Pixels and tags — tiny invisible elements used to measure page visits (only where enabled).
Plain English: Cookies are small files that help PutForm remember who you are and how you like to use the site. They can't read your files or spy on other websites.
Section 02

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a small number of clearly defined purposes:

  • Keeping you signed in — so you don't have to log in on every page you visit.
  • Securing your account — helping us detect suspicious or fraudulent sessions.
  • Remembering preferences — such as dark mode, language, and layout choices.
  • Understanding usage — aggregated, anonymized statistics that show which features are used and where the platform can improve.
  • Protecting the Service — rate limiting and abuse prevention on our API and form submission endpoints.

We do not use cookies to sell your personal data, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on PutForm.

Section 03

Types of Cookies We Use

By duration

  • Session cookies — exist only while your browser is open and are deleted automatically when you close it. Our authentication cookie is a session cookie.
  • Persistent cookies — remain on your device for a set period (or until you delete them). These are used for preferences like theme selection.

By source

  • First-party cookies — set directly by PutForm and used only by us.
  • Third-party cookies — set by external services we rely on, such as infrastructure or error-monitoring providers. These are limited and described in Section 06.

By purpose

  • Strictly necessary — required for the platform to function. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preference — remember choices you make, such as dark mode.
  • Analytics — help us understand aggregate usage. Optional.

Section 05

Session & Authentication Cookies

PutForm uses cookie-based sessions to authenticate users. When you log in, our server issues a session cookie that identifies your account on subsequent requests. This is why you can move between your dashboard, forms, and profile without logging in again.

Because these cookies are essential to how the Service works:

  • They cannot be disabled while you are logged in.
  • They are marked HttpOnly so client-side scripts cannot read them.
  • They are marked Secure so they are only sent over HTTPS connections.
  • They are invalidated immediately when you log out or delete your account.
Important: Never share your session cookie with anyone. Anyone who possesses it can act as you until it expires. PutForm staff will never ask for your session cookie or password.

If you use our public API with server-side credentials, those credentials are sent in request headers and do not rely on browser cookies.

Section 06

Third-Party Cookies

Some parts of PutForm are delivered through trusted third-party providers. These providers may set their own cookies when you interact with their services. We keep this list as short as possible and only work with vendors who process data on our instructions.

  • Infrastructure & hosting — our backend runs on a cloud platform that may set short-lived technical cookies for load balancing and security.
  • Payment processing — if you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment provider may set cookies to prevent fraud and complete checkout securely. We never receive or store your full card details.
  • Error monitoring — used to detect crashes and broken pages. These records are aggregated and do not identify you personally.

We do not permit third parties to use cookies on PutForm for advertising or to build cross-site profiles of your behavior.

Section 07

Managing Your Cookies

You are in control of cookies. You can manage them in two ways: through the preference panel below, and through your browser settings.

Strictly necessary

Required for login, security, and core platform functionality.

Always on

Preferences

Analytics

Anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the product.

Allowed

Through your browser

Every major browser lets you view, block, and delete cookies. Look under Settings → Privacy and security in your browser. Useful starting points:

  • Chrome — "Third-party cookies" and "Site data"
  • Safari — "Prevent cross-site tracking" and "Manage Website Data"
  • Firefox — "Cookies and Site Data"
  • Edge — "Cookies and site permissions"

Blocking all cookies will prevent you from logging in to PutForm, since our authentication relies on a session cookie. Clearing cookies will log you out and reset your saved theme.

Section 08

Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal to websites. There is still no common industry standard for how DNT should be interpreted, so PutForm does not currently respond to DNT headers.

However, because we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, there is no behavioral profile to opt out of in the first place. If a legal standard for DNT is adopted in your jurisdiction, we will revisit this position.

Section 09

Retention Periods

Cookies stay on your device only as long as necessary for the purpose they were set:

  • Session cookies are removed when you close your browser or log out.
  • Preference cookies are kept for up to 12 months, or until you clear them.
  • Analytics cookies are kept for up to 6 months in aggregate form.
  • Your consent record is kept for 12 months before we ask you again.

When you delete your PutForm account, server-side session records are invalidated immediately and any remaining client-side cookies expire on their normal schedule or can be cleared manually from your browser.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove cookies, change providers, or when the law requires it. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or with an in-app notice.

Your continued use of PutForm after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy. If you do not agree, you can adjust your cookie preferences or stop using the Service.
