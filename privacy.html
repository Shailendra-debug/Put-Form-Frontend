Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information PutForm collects, how we use it, and the choices you have as a user of our platform.

Section 01

Introduction

PutForm ("we", "us", "our") provides a form-building platform that lets individuals and teams create forms, collect responses, and integrate submissions into their own products. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information when you use our website, dashboard, API, and any related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using PutForm, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the Service.

Plain English: We collect only what we need to run the platform, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information in three main ways:

Information you give us

  • Account details — name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Form responses — the answers and data your respondents submit through forms you create.
  • Contact messages — name, email, phone, subject, and message when you use our contact form.
  • Profile data — optional details you add to your account, like a company name or profile picture.

Information collected automatically

  • Session data — cookie-based session identifiers used to keep you logged in.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and timestamps of activity.
  • Device data — browser type, operating system, and general location (country level).

Information from third parties

If you sign in using an external identity provider, we may receive your basic profile information (name and email) from that provider to create or link your account.

Section 03

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the PutForm platform.
  • Authenticate your session and keep your account secure.
  • Deliver form responses to the form owner and power the dashboard experience.
  • Respond to your support requests and contact messages.
  • Send service-related notifications such as security alerts or important product changes.
  • Monitor usage patterns to improve performance, fix bugs, and plan new features.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your form responses or contact messages for advertising, and we do not sell them to third parties.

Section 04

Cookies & Sessions

PutForm uses cookie-based sessions to authenticate users. When you log in, the server sets a secure, HTTP-only session cookie that identifies your account for the duration of your session. This cookie is required for the dashboard and authenticated API endpoints to work.

  • Essential cookies — required for login, security, and core functionality.
  • Preference storage — we store your theme choice (light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage.
  • No advertising cookies — we do not use third-party tracking or ad cookies.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings. Doing so will log you out and reset your theme preference, but will not affect any content you've created on PutForm.

Section 05

Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only in limited situations:

  • With your consent — when you explicitly ask us to share data with another service.
  • Service providers — trusted vendors who help us host, secure, and operate the platform (e.g. cloud hosting, email delivery). They are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of PutForm, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — if PutForm is acquired or merged, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before any such change takes effect.

We never sell your personal information or your form responses to advertisers or data brokers.

Section 06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic between your browser and PutForm is encrypted using HTTPS.
  • Session cookies are marked HTTP-only and SameSite to reduce exposure to cross-site attacks.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.

While we work hard to protect your information, no online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@putform.com.

Section 07

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Service. Specifically:

  • Account data — retained until you delete your account.
  • Form responses — retained until the form owner deletes the form or the responses.
  • Contact messages — retained for up to 24 months to help us track recurring issues.
  • Logs and usage data — retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, unless we are required by law to retain it longer.

Section 08

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal information.
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing, such as analytics.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@putform.com. We will respond within 30 days. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

PutForm is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by posting a prominent notice on the platform before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Your continued use of PutForm after the changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
